# Maroon 5: Complete Song List

> **Source note, not the dataset.** This is the research document the Maroon 5
> data was compiled from. The canonical machine-readable data is
> `src/content/maroon-5.json`, which is what the site reads and what CI
> validates. Edit the JSON, not this file. If the two disagree, the JSON wins.

**Version:** 1.0
**Compiled:** August 2026
**Coverage:** All known recordings through August 2026
**Totals:** 148 Maroon 5 releases, 64 Kara's Flowers titles, 3 unreleased. 215 entries.

---

## Scope and Method

**Year** is the year of first commercial release, not the parent album's release year. These differ in several cases. For example "Memories" was released in 2019 but appeared on _Jordi_ in 2021.

**Included:** every recording released under the Maroon 5 name, covering studio albums, deluxe and territory-specific editions, re-releases, compilations, covers, soundtrack contributions, non-album singles, and charting remix singles.

**Excluded:** live versions of songs already listed; all tracks on _Call and Response: The Remix Album_ (2008); guest features by individual members on other artists' records (Adam Levine, PJ Morton, James Valentine, Sam Farrar as sidemen).

**Multi-album songs** occupy a single row with album names comma-separated.

**Kara's Flowers** material is separated into Section B because it predates the Maroon 5 name and the addition of James Valentine, though the core lineup is the same.

### Compilations and EPs containing no new material

These appear in the Album(s) column where relevant but contribute no new songs.

- **The B-Side Collection**, December 18, 2007. Seven _It Won't Be Soon Before Long_ offcuts. Peaked at number 51 on the Billboard 200.
- **Will Be Loved EP**, February 7, 2025. Seven-track Valentine's compilation of existing hits.
- **Singles Collection**, Japan, November 26, 2025. Tracklist unverified.
- **Rhythms del Mundo: Cuba**, November 14, 2006. Afro-Cuban rework of "She Will Be Loved."
- **Call and Response: The Remix Album**, December 9, 2008. Remixes only.

### Residual risk

The only plausible remaining gap is a territory-exclusive bonus track never documented on Wikipedia. Japanese, Korean, and Australian editions are where those hide. Closing this requires a Discogs query by release rather than by song, which is a separate research pass.

### Counting rules

Totals shift depending on your rule:

- 148 Maroon 5 releases as listed
- 143 if the five remix singles are excluded (entries 111, 142, 143, 145, 147)
- 212 including Kara's Flowers
- 215 including unreleased material
